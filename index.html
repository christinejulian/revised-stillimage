1. Departure from Southampton
The RMS Titanic departed from Southampton, England, on April 10, 1912, beginning its maiden voyage to New York.
2. Luxury Onboard
The ship was famous for its elegant interiors, which includes the Grand Staircase, rich cabins, and dining rooms.
3. Life Below Deck
Hundreds of engineers and crew members worked around the clock to keep the huge steamship operating.
4. The Iceberg
On the night of April 14, 1912, lookouts spotted an iceberg directly ahead of the ship’s location. Despite efforts to avoid the iceberg, the ship struck the iceberg.
5. The Distress Call
As water flooded several compartments, distress signals and rockets were sent into the night sky requesting quick help.
6. Launching the Lifeboats
Passengers and crew boarded lifeboats, but many left partially full while leaving hundreds still aboard.
7. Rescue
The RMS Carpathia arrived several hours later and rescued the remaining survivors from the lifeboats.
8. Remembering the Titanic
A century later, the Titanic remains a symbol of human arrogance, tragedy, and the importance of ship and passenger safety.
